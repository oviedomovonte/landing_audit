The problem

Jira configuration builds up quietly. Projects, schemes, workflows and fields, layered over years and spread across admin screens made for editing, not auditing.

Every hour spent clicking through settings to piece the picture together is an hour not spent improving it. AuditBuddy collects the configuration into one view you can explore, question and export.
